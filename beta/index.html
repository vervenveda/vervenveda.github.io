Beta Program

Help us test Khaemenes Academy and the wider Verve N Veda ecosystem. Beta participation is for usability, navigation, curriculum experience, accessibility, and system feedback — never for exposing passwords, private student records, or other sensitive information.

What We Test

Real-world experience

  • navigation and broken links
  • student and family workflow clarity
  • grade and course continuity
  • mobile and print usability
  • accessibility and readability
  • assessment and lesson experience
  • bugs, confusing behavior, and missing resources
Privacy Boundary

Keep feedback non-sensitive

Please do not submit passwords, passcodes, dates of birth, medical information, street addresses, government identifiers, private academic records, or other sensitive child information through this public beta form.

The protected enrollment/account service will handle authenticated family and learner data separately from beta feedback.
