Intelligence & Navigation Layer

NAIB guides the ecosystem.

NAIB is the public-facing intelligence and navigation guide for the Verve N Veda ecosystem—organizing context, coordinating approved specialist pathways, and helping visitors reach the most appropriate doorway without turning every Hall into the same application.

A learning request can route toward Khaemenes Academy; discovery can move through PLERA Search; deeper context can reach ARSHIF; evidence-sensitive questions can use The Verifier; creative and professional work can move toward specialist tools; and connected communication can hand off to 333.

Open NAIB Browse Every Public Hall
01 · Understand Context Recognizes the visitor’s current mode, learning stage, project, and explicit preferences.
02 · Organize Route Directs the request toward the right Verve N Veda Hall, resource, or specialist pathway.
03 · Coordinate Specialists Connects learning, research, verification, creativity, planning, archives, and communication.
04 · Preserve Boundaries Keeps permissions, provenance, privacy, and high-stakes verification separate from simple discovery.

NAIB provides local navigation, memory, resource federation, and verification-aware structure. Secure provider services remain separate from public page credentials.
