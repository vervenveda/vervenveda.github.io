Intelligence & Navigation Layer

NAIB guides the ecosystem.

NAIB is the public-facing intelligence and navigation guide for the Verve N Veda ecosystem—organizing context, coordinating approved specialist pathways, and helping visitors reach the most appropriate doorway without turning every Hall into the same application.

A learning request can route toward Khaemenes Academy; discovery can move through PLERA Search; deeper context can reach ARSHIF; evidence-sensitive questions can use The Verifier; creative and professional work can move toward specialist tools; and connected communication can hand off to 333.
