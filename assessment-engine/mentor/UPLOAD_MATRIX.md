## Safety and routing rules in this wave

- Admin repositories are excluded.
- Candidate/campaign pages are deliberately omitted from educational and civic manifests.
- News, voter-information, search, and network resources carry freshness metadata.
- Faith-specific resources carry `requiresPreferenceMatch` metadata for the future preference-aware resolver.
- 333 Network begins at higher-learning/adult/parent scope rather than general child access.
- Aurora resources are optional reflection/wellness tools and are not described as treatment.
- Directory-style routes were audited where ambiguity existed; known non-landing directories were replaced with direct files.
