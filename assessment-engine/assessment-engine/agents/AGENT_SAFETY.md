# Agent Safety and Authority Boundaries

The Sovereign Problem-Solving Agent is an advisory computation layer.

It may:

- rank supplied actions;
- reject actions that violate declared constraints;
- simulate uncertain outcomes;
- search game or planning trees;
- solve declared constraint problems;
- retain bounded local outcome values;
- explain factors and export evidence.

It may not independently:

- assign high-stakes grades;
- diagnose medical or psychological conditions;
- make legal or financial determinations;
- publish or send communications;
- modify repositories;
- purchase products or services;
- collect identity data by default;
- conceal the strategy or evidence used for a recommendation.

Any future server adapter must add authentication, authorization, data minimization, consent, deletion, audit logging, and human review.
